Text & Email Terms and Privacy

How we use the information you share with us

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Reach The World Ministries (RTWM) offers a free text messaging and email program to anyone who signs up through our website. Messages typically include the weekly Prophetic Word (Tongues & Interpretation), occasional sermon links, and, from time to time, an invitation to support the ministry.

Consent & Opt-In

By checking the sign-up box on our Contact page or another RTWM sign-up form, you agree to receive recurring text messages and/or emails from Reach The World Ministries at the phone number and/or email address you provide. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service, and you may withdraw it at any time.

Message Frequency

You can expect about one message a week, with occasional additional messages around special content or ministry updates.

Message and data rates may apply. Rates depend on your mobile carrier plan, not on Reach The World Ministries.

Opt-Out & Help

Reply STOP to any text message at any time to stop receiving texts from us. Reply HELP for assistance, or reach us directly at gbrtwm@gmail.com. You can unsubscribe from email using the link at the bottom of any email we send.

Information We Collect

When you sign up, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, phone number, and/or email address.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to send you the content you signed up for — the weekly word, sermon links, and occasional ministry updates — and to respond if you contact us directly.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or email address with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your information is used solely by Reach The World Ministries to communicate with you.

Data Security & Retention

We store your information securely and keep it only as long as needed to provide this service. You may ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent version.
