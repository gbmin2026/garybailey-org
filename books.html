Make Your Church a Holy Ghost Church

Follow after Charity, and Desire Spiritual Gifts

A Holy Ghost church is a church where the Word is honored, the Spirit is welcomed, Jesus is glorified, and the people of God are edified.

In Make Your Church a Holy Ghost Church, Gary B. Bailey gives a clear and practical call for pastors, leaders, and believers to return to the New Testament pattern for the gathered church. With Scripture as the foundation, this book addresses the baptism with the Holy Ghost, the manifestations of the Spirit, tongues and interpretation, prophecy, spiritual order, and the need for churches to make room for the present ministry of the Holy Ghost.

This is not a call to confusion or excess. It is a call to the Word and the Spirit working together, so the Church may be strengthened according to God’s design.

“Let all things be done unto edifying.” — 1 Corinthians 14:26

Available now in paperback on Amazon.

About the Author
