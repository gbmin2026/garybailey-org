Welcome

Preaching the Gospel, building up the Church

For decades Gary Bailey has served as a pastor and evangelist — carrying the Gospel of Jesus Christ across the country and to the nations through revivals, church meetings, and overseas missions.

His heart is to encourage and strengthen God’s people: to stir up faith, to make room for the presence and gifts of the Holy Spirit, and to see believers rise up strong so the work of God may be built in the earth.
